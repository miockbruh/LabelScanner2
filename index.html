Hidden exposures — never on the label
BPA, phthalates, PFAS, solvent residues, heavy metals and microplastics aren't ingredients — they migrate from packaging, form during processing, or contaminate the supply chain. No scanner can read them off a label, so know the routes instead. Where brands are named: a recall means the company acted; published testing means measured results; a lawsuit "alleges" means a claim not yet proven. Products reformulate — treat brand lists as dated snapshots. When a barcode lookup includes packaging data, matching cards get marked.
